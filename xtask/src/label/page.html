Labelling

Mark each document or photo by clicking its four corners: top-left of the upright item first, then clockwise (top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left). The yellow edge is the top. Drag a handle, or select it and use the arrow keys (Shift = 10 px, Alt = fine). Corners may lie outside the picture for partial frames.

Then tick the slice tags (at least one) and flags. A label is saved automatically as soon as it is valid. Only your own judgement goes in: no suggestion is shown unless you ask for it below, and then the label is marked assisted and excluded from the golden evaluation.
