Labelling

Mark each document or photo by clicking its four corners: top-left of the upright item first, then clockwise (top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left). The yellow edge is the top. Drag a handle, or select it and use the arrow keys (Shift = 10 px, Alt = fine). Corners may lie outside the picture for partial frames.

Then tick the slice tags (at least one) and flags. A label is saved automatically as soon as it is valid. Only your own judgement goes in: no suggestion is shown unless you ask for it below, and then the label is marked assisted and excluded from the golden evaluation.

Curved edges (a crumpled receipt, a bowed page): after the four corners are placed, press C or tick Curved edges mode. Each edge shows a round handle at its middle: drag it to bend the edge into a smooth curve. Double-click or long-press on a curve adds another point; double-click a point or press Delete removes it; [ / ] select the previous / next handle and the arrow keys nudge it; P adds a point; Esc deselects. Straighten flattens one edge, Reset all curves flattens all four. The dashed blue outline is the straight quad; the pink lines are the curves. Moving a corner carries the edge with it (its bend scales and turns with the edge).
