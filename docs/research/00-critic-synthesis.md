GAPS:
- Linux touch and pinch behaviour on current WebKitGTK (Wayland and X11, NVIDIA and Intel) is unmeasured in every source, and my own search budget ran out before I could check. The Slint fallback is also weaker than presented. Slint's default winit backend has no OS file or folder drop in 1.18.1, and the only workaround (winit 0.30 DroppedFile events) is untested on Wayland. Follow-up: a week-1 spike that tests folder drop, a 24 MP pinch/pan/rotate at ~60 fps, HiDPI and dark mode on BOTH Tauri and Slint, on a Surface, a MacBook and a Linux touchscreen.
- Nothing is measured. Every budget is extrapolated from Ryzen 5950X/7950X or 2008-2017 CPUs, with no Apple silicon, no Tier-M laptop, and no Tauri IPC or custom-URI tile latency. The 84 ms turbojpeg-per-6MP figure is unsourced. The stage budgets also do not add up (see conflicts). Follow-up: criterion plus gungraun benchmarks on real 12, 48 and 100 MP files before any number becomes a promise.
- HEIC is unresolved on four fronts. There is no legal opinion on HEVC patents for a free decoder. The price and availability of the Windows HEVC extension is unverified. OS decoders (ImageIO, WIC) are unbenchmarked. Colour handling (Display P3 as ICC or nclx, PQ/HLG tone-mapping, gain maps) is unchecked against real iPhone and Android files. - Model supply is unsolved. Long narrow receipts (aspect above 4:1), thermal fade, partial frames and touching items are under-represented in SmartDoc, MIDV and CORD. A 256x256 heatmap net may cope poorly with 8:1 aspect. - Whole areas were not researched: PDF in and out and OCR; CLI surface; scanner and camera capture; watch folders; OS integration ('Open with', file associations, Share/Send To); Windows-on-ARM; screen-reader testing on a webview canvas; localisation workflow. CONFLICTS:
- [gui vs ux (vs priorart)] The GUI report picks Tauri 2 and the UX report picks Slint (Qt as viable). The UX pick rests on multi-touch, but Slint has no RTL mirroring (issue #2294 open), its OS folder drop is Qt-only, and it lacks a mature quad-editor and loupe. A web UI gives better RTL, IME, accessibility and design-system reach, but weaker Linux touch. => Tauri 2 + Svelte is primary. Slint is a gated fallback, not a co-equal option. Linux is first-class for mouse, trackpad and keyboard, and touch is validated in the spike. Keep all pixel work and history in a UI-agnostic core crate so a shell swap costs a UI rewrite only.
- [oss vs formats/arch/priorart (licence)] OSS recommends GPL-3.0-or-later (Slint free tier, borrowing Scan Tailor Advanced ideas). Formats, arch and priorart recommend MIT OR Apache-2.0. With Tauri primary, Slint no longer forces GPL. Slint's royalty-free terms even allow an MIT app with attribution, though they are non-OSI and may clash with SignPath's 'no proprietary components' rule. Scan Tailor's 4lex4 fork is stale, so the code-reuse argument is weak. => Follow the user's answer to (b). Every dependency in the candidate stack is compatible with either choice. Rule out AGPL (heic, dssim-core), GPL (jpegxl-rs, x265/x264) and non-commercial (DocTr, DocEnTr) code. Reimplement GPL ideas clean-room from papers. Enforce with cargo-deny.
- [gui vs perf (preview architecture)] Perf budgets a GPU homography drag preview at 8 ms per frame and a 16 ms slider tick. GUI warns against a GLSL preview plus a separate Rust implementation because they will drift. A webview cannot share a GPU texture with Rust, and three different proxy sizes appear (1024 px detect, ~1.5k enhance, 2-4 MP display). => One authoritative pixel path in Rust. During a drag, the webview applies a geometry-only CSS/canvas matrix transform to the already loaded proxy (no IPC). On release, Rust renders the real result and it cross-fades in. Slider budget becomes 16 ms compute in Rust and about 50 ms round-trip. Fix one pyramid: 256 px thumbnail, 1024 px detection, ~1.5k analysis, 2-4 MP display proxy plus 512 px tiles.
- [formats vs arch vs perf (decode isolation)] Formats wants every decode in a sandboxed helper process. Arch defers a subprocess worker. Perf wants fast in-process scaled decode for first paint. catch_unwind does not stop segfaults in libheif, PDFium or LibRaw, and rayon aborts on panics in spawned tasks. => Use a persistent worker-process pool (shared-memory pixel return) from v1 for C parsers of untrusted input: libheif, PDFium, LibRaw. Keep zune-jpeg, png, tiff and turbojpeg in-process behind hard limits (pixel cap, alloc cap, timeout), per-item catch_unwind and panic=unwind. Enforce our own caps, because image's default alloc limit is non-strict.
- [formats vs oss vs arch (HEIC engine)] Three ways to get HEIC are each flawed. libheif-sys embedded-libheif is static, has no plugins, is about four releases behind, and forces x264/x265 detection. On Windows it always uses vcpkg, whose default `hevc` feature pulls GPL x265. Pure-Rust decoders are AGPL or under six months old. OS codecs are inconsistent (Windows needs a paid or OEM HEVC extension). Flatpak's runtime builds libheif without libde265. => A HeicBackend trait: ImageIO on macOS and WIC on Windows first, then our own decode-only libheif >=1.23.5 CMake build (no x265/x264, libheif tracked within days of advisories) with libde265 as a separate optional plugin. Do not use libheif-sys embedded mode. The Flatpak bundles its own libde265. Re-evaluate the MIT/Apache pure-Rust heic-rs in about six months.
- [perf vs geometry vs enhance (budget stack-up)] Perf budgets 400 ms for a full 12 MP auto-process and at least 10 images per second on 6 cores. Geometry estimates 250-450 ms without enhancement; enhance adds 60-250 ms; decode is about 85 ms plus a JPEG encode of about 100 ms. The parts sum above 400 ms, and the batch target looks about 2x optimistic. All are unmeasured. => Treat all budgets as provisional. Change the provisional targets to at most 700 ms single image at 12 MP, at least 4-5 images per second on 6 cores, and keep the at most 40 ms proxy analysis and at most 150 ms first overlay. Re-baseline after the Tier-M spike. Keep the lossless JPEG fast path for 90-degree rotates and MCU-aligned crops.
- [ux vs perf vs formats (HEIC colour default)] UX defaults HEIC to JPG with Display P3 converted to sRGB. Perf defaults to preserving ICC/P3 with an sRGB toggle. Formats says embed P3 or convert. A checker also disagrees on whether iPhone stills carry an ICC profile or only an nclx tag. => Default HEIC to JPG for compatibility, converted to sRGB with an ICC-embedded fallback, with a visible 'preserve wide gamut' toggle. Read the ICC ('prof') first, then nclx/CICP. Tone-map HDR to SDR with a notice. Note dropped gain maps, depth and Live Photo video.
- [geometry vs enhance vs arch (pure-Rust kernel gaps)] The 'pure-Rust, no OpenCV' plan needs kernels that are missing or unsafe as shipped. imageproc has no Lanczos warp, no Sauvola or CLAHE, and its u32 integral images overflow above ~16.8 MP. kornia's Lanczos warp is f32 only (12 MP RGB f32 is about 144 MB). turbojpeg-sys needs CMake and NASM, so 'no C toolchain' is false. There is no Rust LSD crate, and the original LSD may be AGPL. => Spike kornia-imgproc's Lanczos warp first, then write a strip-wise u8/u16 Lanczos warp. Write Sauvola with u64 strip integrals. Port LSD only from OpenCV's BSD-style version, or use EDLines. Ship a devcontainer and xtask for the native toolchain. Use opencv-rust as a dev-only test oracle, never shipped.
